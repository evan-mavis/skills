# Integrations

Integrations lets retailers connect a POS and lets sellers connect commerce, inventory, and fulfillment platforms, then configure sync behavior, link or import products, monitor connection state, and recover from expired access.

## Sub-features

- `buyer-integrations` covers the retailer hub at `/account/buyer/integrations`, provider detail at `/account/buyer/integrations/<providerConfigKey>`, and the connected POS product workspace at `/pos/<providerConfigKey>`.
- `buyer-pos-configuration` covers location linking, manual or automatic product creation and SKU matching, grouped or split variants, activation on delivery, brand-name inclusion, and inventory updates on delivery.
- `buyer-pos-products` covers `All`, `Linked`, and `Unlinked` product views; search; external catalog linking; import to POS; update; inventory view or adjustment; publish or unpublish; unlink; and delete where the provider advertises those capabilities.
- `seller-integrations` covers the seller hub at `/account/seller/integrations`, provider detail at `/account/seller/integrations/<providerConfigKey>`, and product linking at `/account/seller/integrations/<providerConfigKey>/products`.
- `seller-sync-configuration` covers order and sample export, account-wide auto-accept, inventory and pricing sync, pricing markdown, Shopify inventory location, case-versus-unit conversion, Shopify order tags and numbering, ShipStation store selection, and Cin7 sale defaults.
- `seller-product-sync` covers initial catalog sync, manual and automatic SKU linking, provider-to-Airgoods product import, and explicit inventory or pricing refresh.
- `connection-lifecycle` covers Nango Connect, connected/not-enabled state, connection test, disconnect, expired-credential reconnect, and seller migration from legacy API keys.
- `integration-request` covers a missing POS, accounting, fulfillment, or commerce integration request. Accounting is a request category; there is no accounting connection workspace in this surface.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Sign in as a retailer and choose `Integrations` in account navigation, or open `/account/buyer/integrations`. Available cards can include `Square`, `Lightspeed`, and `Clover`; `Toast` is a non-interactive `Coming soon!` card. `Shopify` POS is also presented as coming soon outside the testing environment, even when its catalog row is enabled.
- Choose an available retailer card to open `/account/buyer/integrations/<providerConfigKey>`, where the provider key is `squareup`, `lightspeed-retail`, or `clover`. A connected provider also adds `<Display name> Integration` to account navigation and exposes `/pos/<providerConfigKey>`.
- Sign in as a seller and choose `Integrations`, or open `/account/seller/integrations`. The current hub can include Shopify (`shopify-cc` for a new seller, or the seller's existing `shopify` or `shopify-api-key` connection), `shipstation`, `woocommerce`, `bigcommerce`, and `cin7-core`.
- Choose a seller card to open `/account/seller/integrations/<providerConfigKey>`. On a connected provider with product linking, choose `Link products` to reach `/account/seller/integrations/<providerConfigKey>/products`.
- Legacy `/account/buyer/settings/integrations/...` and `/account/seller/settings/integrations/...` URLs redirect to the corresponding main-nav routes while preserving the remaining path, query, and hash.
- When the seller has `integration-legacy-v1` enabled, `/account/seller/integrations` shows the API-key table instead of the card hub. Existing Shopify or ShipStation keys can show `Upgrade Shopify integration`, `Upgrade now`, or `Migrate both now`; `/account/seller/integrations/product-sync-v1` is the legacy ShipStation product-sync page.

## Driving it

Preconditions:

- Full mode doctor passes. Redis and the queue worker must be healthy for Nango webhook ingestion, initial product or inventory sync, seller bulk import, and buyer order-created or order-delivered automation.
- Follow `Authenticated setup` in `../SKILL.md`. Use `$query-local-db` to select one buyer with a non-null `store_id` or one seller with a non-null `supplier_id`, then confirm the selected owner has the provider and state needed for the recipe.
- Before a connected-state drive, confirm an `integration` row with the expected `provider_config_key`, correct `store` or `supplier` availability, and `coming_soon=false`; a canonical `integration_connection` row for that owner with `status='connected'`, a non-null Nango connection ID, and provider-shaped settings; and, for populated product flows, connection-scoped `integration_product_data` plus appropriate product links. Seller order-sync proof additionally needs eligible linked variants and order-link/order-data state.
- A real connection or fresh sync also requires a designated provider sandbox account, valid Nango host and secret, the matching provider configuration and scopes, and inbound Nango webhook delivery to this backend. If any are absent, verify only branch-local read states and report connect or sync as unreachable.
- Record provider key, role, owner ID, connection ID, initial settings, product/link counts, feature-flag value, Nango environment label, queue health, and whether outbound integration exports are enabled. Never record credentials, tokens, store secrets, or raw provider payloads.

- **Hub states.** Open the role's integrations route. Require heading `Integrations` and the description `Connect your external platforms to sync products, inventory, and orders.` Wait for the three skeleton cards to settle. Require provider cards with `Enabled`, `Not enabled`, or `Coming soon!`, plus `Request an integration`. An empty catalog may legitimately leave only the request card; a failed catalog request can leave the grid empty, so retain the failed response and do not call that a verified empty state.
- **Integration request.** Choose `Request an integration`; require dialog `Request an integration`, `Integration category`, and `Submit`. Retailer categories are `POS`, `Accounting`, and `Other`; seller categories are `Fulfillment / Shipping`, `E-commerce Platform`, `Accounting`, and `Other`. Verify category/tool dependent fields and cancel. Submitting writes a persistent request and may emit tracking/error reporting, so submit only when explicitly scoped and remove the branch-local request afterward.
- **Not connected detail.** Choose a card or open the exact provider detail route. Require heading equal to the card name, button `Back`, button `Connect`, heading `Overview`, and `Getting Started` when catalog content supplies it. `Setup Guide`, `Video Tutorial`, provider website, and screenshot links leave Airgoods; check their `href` without opening unless external navigation is required.
- **Connect.** With an explicitly authorized sandbox provider, choose `Connect` and require the control to become `Connecting...`, then complete the Nango-hosted Connect UI with sandbox credentials. Success must close the Connect UI, toast `Integration connected successfully`, show `Connected`, and expose connected content after refetch. A closed or blocked window is cancellation, not success. For Shopify client credentials, bad values must surface `We couldn't connect to Shopify. Check that your Client ID and Client Secret are correct, then try again.` Missing scopes or provider validation must remain a failed connection. Confirm the new connected row and eventual product sync through a read-only query; do not expose the Nango connection ID in evidence.
- **Initial sync.** On a newly connected product-sync provider, require `Syncing products...` or `Syncing products from <provider>...` and `This may take a few minutes.` The page polls until completion or at least one product exists and may trigger one recovery sync. Require queue processing and a settled product count before claiming success. After three minutes with no products, accept the explicit `Products from <provider> have not synced yet. Check your connection in Settings and try again.` error as a failed/recovery state.
- **Buyer configuration.** Open `/account/buyer/integrations/<providerConfigKey>` for a connected retailer. Require `Connected`, `Products`, `Manage products`, and provider-supported cards such as `Location linking`, `Product linking`, `Product creation`, and `Inventory management`. `Test connection` is intentionally absent for the configured POS providers. For a reversible settings check, change exactly one option such as `Edit before adding`/`Add automatically`, `Manually`/`Automatically`, `Group variants`/`Split variants`, `Activate products on delivery`, or `Include brand name`; wait for the saved state to refetch, prove it with a read-only query, then restore the original value.
- **Buyer locations.** In `Location linking`, accept skeletons, `No store locations found.`, `No <provider> locations found.`, `Locations not linked`, `Some locations not linked`, or the paired `Airgoods Locations` and `<provider> Locations` lists. A `Link` choice and `Unlink` both mutate branch-local mapping data, fetch live provider locations, and can emit external notifications. If scoped, link only a disposable Airgoods location to a sandbox provider location, require `Location linked successfully`, then `Unlink` and require `Location unlinked successfully`; confirm the original mapping is restored. `Add Location` creates account data and needs separate cleanup.
- **Buyer POS products.** Choose `Manage products` or open `/pos/<providerConfigKey>`. An unconnected provider must toast `Please connect your <provider> first` and return to the hub. A status-check failure must show `Failed to check connection status. Please try again.` and `Back to Integrations`. Connected state requires heading `<Display name> Integration`, button `Settings`, tabs `All`, `Linked`, `Unlinked`, and input `Search products...`. Accept product cards, `Order products for them to appear here`, or `No <status> products found`.
- **Buyer product actions.** Use capability-visible labels only: `Import to <provider>`, `Link to <provider>`, `View inventory`, `Update product`, `Publish on <provider>`, `Unpublish from <provider>`, `Unlink from <provider>`, or `Delete from <provider>`. Linking/unlinking changes Airgoods mappings; import, update, inventory adjustment, publish/unpublish, and delete write to the sandbox provider, and delete warns that it cannot be undone. Default to opening and cancelling the modal. If mutation is explicitly scoped, capture both systems' initial state, perform one sandbox mutation, require the exact success toast, verify through a second user-facing read and a read-only query, then restore both systems. Never use delete as a reversibility test.
- **Seller detail.** Open a connected seller provider. Require `Connected`, `Product Link`, a linked count or `No products linked`, settings, and `Test Connection` except on ShipStation. `No products linked` must also show the warning that integration functionality is unavailable until products are linked. Choose `Test connection` only against an authorized sandbox; require `Connection is working`, provider failure text, or `Failed to test connection`.
- **Seller sync settings.** Verify only sections supported by the provider. `Sync settings` can expose `Export orders`, `Auto-accept orders`, `Sync samples`, `Auto-accept samples`, `Sync inventory`, `Sync pricing`, and `Markdown`. Shopify can expose `Inventory location`, `Order customization`, `Order tags`, order-number options, and `Order discount`; ShipStation exposes `<provider> store selection`, `Loading stores...`, `Try again`, `No stores found in your ShipStation account.`, `Reload stores`, or a `Select a store` control; Cin7 exposes `Cin7 sale defaults`, `Loading options from Cin7…`, `Try again`, `Refresh options from Cin7`, warehouse, tax, payment-term, price-tier, and optional account controls. Settings save immediately. Change and restore one field only; note that auto-accept is account-wide, not provider-specific.
- **Inventory-source conflict.** If another seller connection owns inventory sync, require disabled inventory control and warning `Inventory sync unavailable` naming the owner. A legacy multiple-owner state must show `Multiple inventory sources active`. Do not force either state by enabling conflicting live integrations; use a prepared branch-local fixture.
- **Seller products.** Choose `Link products`. Require heading `Product Link`, buttons `Import Products`, `Integration Settings`, and `Link Automatically`; require `Sync Products` only when inventory or pricing sync is enabled. Accept first-sync loader, stats, or the product table with `Airgoods Product`, `Link`, `Unlink`, and `Search product or SKU...`. `Link Automatically` must open `Link Products Automatically`; cancel by default. `Sync Products` must open `Sync Products with <provider>`; cancel by default because confirmation requests fresh provider data and can overwrite Airgoods inventory or pricing according to settings.
- **Seller import.** Open `Import Products` and require `Import Products from <provider>`, `Search products`, `Select all`, filter choices `All`, `On Airgoods`, and `Not on Airgoods`, and `Import 0 selected`. Accept row skeletons, provider rows, `No products available to import`, `No products match your search`, or `No <provider> products found`. Already linked rows show `Already on Airgoods` and are disabled. Submitting creates Airgoods products asynchronously; if explicitly scoped, select one disposable sandbox product, require `Import started for 1 product(s).`, retain the progress panel until completion, verify the new product/link, then remove all created branch-local records.
- **Seller order export.** `Create test order` appears only when the provider supports order export and `sync_orders` is on. Opening it must show `Create Test Order`; cancel by default. Creating writes an Airgoods test order and may export to every connected, order-export-capable provider. Actual provider export requires `INTEGRATION_EXPORT_ENABLED=true`; when false, the Airgoods order can exist without appearing in the provider. Run only with explicit authorization, linked in-stock sandbox variants, recorded connected providers, and a cleanup plan in every affected provider.
- **Disconnect and reconnect.** Open `Connected`, choose `Disconnect...`, and require dialog `Disconnect Integration` with `Disconnect` and `Cancel`. Cancel is the safe default. Disconnect revokes/deletes the Nango connection, stops sync, clears integration-sourced inventory, removes product links, updates account-wide auto-accept when no exporter remains, and emits a connection notification; despite the dialog claiming links are preserved, treat it as destructive and do not confirm on a valued fixture. For a prepared disposable connection, confirm, require `Integration disconnected successfully` and `Connect`, then reconnect the same sandbox provider and wait for a fresh sync. Provider API failures marked for recovery must show an error toast with action `Reconnect`; choosing it follows the legacy settings URL and redirects to the provider detail.
- **Legacy seller migration.** With `integration-legacy-v1` enabled and matching legacy key rows, require the appropriate Shopify/ShipStation migration banner and confirmation dialog. Cancel by default. Confirmation deletes legacy API-key connections, changes the routed experience, and can require a fresh Nango connection; it is not reversible from the web UI. Verify only with explicit authorization and disposable branch-local keys.
- **Proof.** Capture before/after accessibility snapshots and a screenshot showing the role, route, provider name, stable control, and result. For API-backed state, retain the relevant response and a read-only database confirmation. For async work, retain queue progress plus the settled UI. Redact owner and provider secrets and avoid provider customer/order data.

## Gotchas

- Integrations are unavailable in public-only mode and require the correct account role. Do not reuse a buyer session for seller routes or vice versa.
- Card contents come from the selected database. Missing or stale migrations, wrong availability, `coming_soon=true`, unsupported capabilities, or a non-canonical connection can change the route and controls even when frontend code is unchanged.
- Shopify POS is forced to `Coming soon!` outside the testing environment. Do not bypass the non-interactive card and claim it is generally available.
- Seller `integration-legacy-v1` reverses the expected hub: true means legacy API keys; false means the Nango card flow. Existing Shopify connection type also decides whether the seller sees `shopify-api-key`, `shopify`, or `shopify-cc`.
- Nango Connect is a hosted iframe. The selected browser harness may not be able to inspect its inner accessibility tree, and pop-up blocking, manual MFA, CAPTCHA, or missing sandbox credentials is a blocker requiring user takeover. Never paste secrets into evidence.
- A successful OAuth window is not proof of a usable integration. Require the Airgoods connected row, required scopes, queue/webhook delivery, and settled provider data. Conversely, a connected row with a missing or expired Nango connection can render `Connected` until a provider call fails.
- Initial sync and provider-to-Airgoods imports are asynchronous. Backend and web health without Redis and the queue worker is insufficient; remote Nango also needs a webhook path that reaches this run.
- Capabilities control almost every action. Absence of `Link products`, `Sync Products`, `Test connection`, product actions, locations, pricing, inventory, or order export can be correct for that provider.
- Seller inventory sync has one authoritative integration. Never enable a second source just to demonstrate the warning.
- `INTEGRATION_EXPORT_ENABLED` is false by default outside production. Turning it on can create real provider orders; do not change it during routine verification.
- Settings are immediate mutations. Pricing markdown rejects `0` with `Must be at least 1% - 0% would set all your product prices to $0.` ShipStation may auto-select its `Manual Orders` store when no store is saved. Cin7 may auto-fill its default location and payment terms after options load. Snapshot values before opening these pages and restore unexpected writes.
- Product and location link/unlink operations can emit integration-channel notifications even when the data is branch-local. Admin impersonation suppresses selected analytics but does not suppress provider calls, Slack notifications, queue jobs, or database writes; it cannot prove analytics or notification behavior.
- Disconnect copy says product links are preserved, but the disconnect flow removes them. Treat disconnect as destructive until that mismatch is resolved.
- Buyer import/update/inventory/publish/delete, seller product sync, and seller order export call external systems. A branch-local database does not make those provider-side effects local.
- Legacy migration and API-key deletion force reloads and remove credentials. Do not use either as a reversible smoke test.
