# Seller growth tools
Seller growth tools let brands create discounts, join promotional events, run email campaigns, manage retailer relationships, and share Airgoods Direct links and widgets.

## Sub-features

- `seller-discounts` covers the discount list, status filters, search, storewide, product-specific, Buy X Get Y, tiered, and shipping discount create/edit flows.
- `seller-promo-events` covers read-only Sample Box discount reporting and eligible promotional-event opt-in or edit flows.
- `seller-marketing` covers aggregate delivery analytics, campaign lists, draft composition, templates, final review, scheduling, test sends, live sends, and sent-campaign engagement.
- `seller-retailer-crm` covers retailer list filters, search, individual add/edit, detail pages, bulk actions, lifecycle state, relationship stats, recent activity, and orders.
- `seller-retailer-segments` covers filter-based and selected-retailer segments, segment details, and campaign handoff.
- `seller-retailer-notes` covers all-retailer and single-retailer notes in list or grid view.
- `seller-retailer-upload` covers file selection, column mapping, confirmation, queued import progress, failures, duplicates, invalid rows, and segment creation.
- `seller-direct` covers referral invites, Direct links, sample links, QR codes, referral stats, and public Direct-page entry.
- `seller-widgets` covers widget size/color preview, embed-code copy, developer email, and the customer-visible embedded widget.
- `seller-growth-state` covers lifecycle filters, aggregate campaign/referral/discount statistics, sent-email engagement, optional retailer recommendations, and analytics events.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Sign in as a seller. The account menu exposes `Airgoods Direct` at `/account/seller/referrals` and, after onboarding, `Retailers` at `/account/seller/retailers`. Other growth routes may be opened directly.
- Visit `/account/seller/discounts` or `/account/seller/discounts/promotions`. Choose `Create Discount`, then `Storewide Discount`, `Product Specific Discount`, `Buy X Get Y`, `Tiered Discount`, or `Shipping Discount`. Non-tiered choices route to `/account/seller/discounts/create?type=<storewide|product_specific|buy_x_get_y|shipping>`; existing standard discounts use `/account/seller/discounts/<id>`.
- Open `/account/seller/discounts/tiered`, `/account/seller/discounts/sample-box`, or `/account/seller/discounts/promo-events` for consolidated programs. A promo-event deep link may use `/account/seller/discounts/promo-events?optInEventId=<id>`.
- Visit `/account/seller/marketing` for aggregate stats, `/account/seller/marketing/campaigns` for the list, `/account/seller/marketing/campaigns/new` to compose, `/account/seller/marketing/campaigns/<id>` to review, and `/account/seller/marketing/campaigns/<id>/engagement` for a sent campaign.
- Visit `/account/seller/retailers`; open a row at `/account/seller/retailers/<id>`, `View Segments` at `/account/seller/retailers/segments`, or `/account/seller/retailers/segments/<id>`. All-retailer notes are at `/account/seller/retailers/notes`.
- Choose `Add Retailers` for one retailer or `Upload Retailers` for `/account/seller/retailers/upload`. An active import uses `?jobId=<id>`; campaign handoff uses `?fromCampaign=<id>`.
- Legacy `/account/seller/customers`, `/customers/segments`, `/customers/notes`, `/customers/upload`, and child-ID paths must replace `customers` with `retailers` while preserving the query string and hash.
- Visit `/account/seller/referrals/widgets` for the widget builder. `View My Direct Page` opens `/u/<saved-slug>` in a new tab; the sample link is `/u/<saved-slug>/sample`.

## Driving it

Preconditions:

- Full mode doctor and `Authenticated setup` pass with a seller whose user has a non-null supplier. Prefer a seller that is not in onboarding so the complete account navigation is visible.
- Use `/estack-devin:query-local-db` against the verified isolated database to select or create branch-local fixtures. Record seller email and supplier ID; retailer/customer, store, segment, discount, promo-event, upload-job, and campaign IDs used; initial lifecycle/status values; supplier status; saved slug; domain state; and every mutation.
- No PostHog flag gates the core discounts, campaigns, CRM, Direct, or widget routes. The optional retailer growth banner may vary with seller demo flag and placement availability; record those resolved states only when asserting that banner.
- For CRM list/detail coverage, select separate fixtures for an uploaded prospect, invited prospect, signed-up retailer, sampled retailer, and ordered retailer as available. Detail proof should include one prospect and one on-Airgoods retailer.
- Campaign composition requires at least one non-empty retailer segment. Sending or test-email delivery requires a supplier status other than `TEST`, `ON_PAUSE`, or `CHURNED`. More than 1,000 unique recipients requires the seller's own verified domain; smaller sends may use the Airgoods domain.
- Promo-event opt-in requires an event currently offered to this supplier, seller opt-in enabled, a future event start, and an unexpired opt-in window. Sample Box reporting requires generated Sample Box discounts. Direct and widget proof require a non-empty saved supplier slug and supplier UUID.
- Keep the queue worker healthy for retailer imports, campaign dispatch, invites/reminders, and developer emails. Actual email proof also requires enabled test-safe email delivery; never infer delivery from a browser success toast.

- **Discount list.** Open `/account/seller/discounts/promotions`; require heading `Discounts` and `Create Discount`. Exercise `All`, `Active`, `Scheduled`, `Expired`, and `Drafts`, plus search. Wait for loading rows to resolve to named rows or `Click the Create button in the top right corner to add a promotion`. Consolidated Sample Box, promotional-event, and tiered rows appear only on page 1 with no search under `All` or `Active`.
- **Discount form.** Open `Create Discount`; require dialog title `Select discount type` and the five named choices. Drive one choice only as far as its form, then require the matching `Create ...` heading, `All discounts`, form sections, `Summary`, and `Create discount`. Safe validation probes include blank name/code, zero value, no product, or multiple Buy X Get Y products; require the matching visible error. Creating or saving is allowed only with a uniquely named branch-local fixture and a recorded delete/restore plan. Success returns to `/account/seller/discounts/promotions`; edit-load failure shows `Failed to load discount` and also returns there.
- **Tiered discounts.** Open `/account/seller/discounts/tiered`; wait for the spinner to resolve, then require `Tiered Discounts`, `Subtotal Tiered Discount`, `Free Shipping Threshold`, `Summary`, `Active`, and `Save tiered discounts`. `Add tier` and `Add free shipping threshold` change only local form state until save. Do not save without first recording all current tiers and active flags; restore them exactly after proof.
- **Sample Box discounts.** Open `/account/seller/discounts/sample-box`; require `Sample Box Discounts` and either month/program rows or `No Sample Box discounts found.`. For a populated row, expand its customer count and wait through `Loading stores...` to store chips or `No stores attached`. Check `Total boxes`, `Total sales`, `Total customers`, and `View Boxes`. This page is read-only.
- **Promotional events.** Open `/account/seller/discounts/promo-events`; require `Promotional Events` and either `No Promotional Events found.` or event rows with `Applies to`, coverage, sales/upcoming state, and summary totals. For an eligible offer, `Join Event` or `Restore opt-in` opens `<event> Opt-in`; verify `Event window`, `Airgoods covers`, `Your discount`, `Cancel`, and `Save`. Probe zero or over-100 combined coverage for visible validation. Saving creates or changes a buyer-visible discount; do it only in isolated data and restore the prior opt-in/value.
- **Marketing overview and list.** Open `/account/seller/marketing`; require `Marketing`, `View Campaigns`, `Create campaign`, `Recipient engagement`, `Delivery`, and domain state `Domain connected` or `Send emails from your own domain`. Wait for stat spinners to resolve. On `/account/seller/marketing/campaigns`, require `Campaigns`, `Create Campaign`, and `All`, `Draft`, `Scheduled`, `Sent`; exercise a filter and refresh. A read failure that leaves indefinite loading or no explicit error is a failed drive, not an empty state.
- **Campaign draft.** Open `/account/seller/marketing/campaigns/new`. Require steps `Select a campaign name and your recipients`, `Enter your subject and sender details`, and `Create your email campaign`. Select a non-empty segment, enter `Campaign name`, check `Sending to N unique customers`, then verify `Email subject`, `Email preview text`, `From name`, `From address`, `Use template`, `Send Test Email`, and `Continue to final review`. A campaign name can trigger debounced draft autosave and replace `/new` with a persisted campaign ID; use a unique synthetic draft and delete it during cleanup.
- **Campaign final review.** Require the preview plus `From name`, `From address`, `Recipients`, `Email Subject`, `Schedule Send`, and `Send Now`. `Schedule Send` must open `Schedule Campaign`, require `Select a date`, `Timezone`, and `Schedule campaign to N customers`; a past time must show the explicit warning and keep the action disabled. Save-for-later is the default reversible proof. Test send, schedule, send now, delete, and template-request actions are external, queued, persistent, or irreversible and require explicit authorization.
- **Campaign success and engagement.** Draft success is `Campaign created successfully` or `Campaign saved successfully`; schedule/send success uses the corresponding visible success message and returns to the list. For an existing sent campaign, require `View Engagement`, then `/engagement` with the campaign name, recipient count/percentage, `All`, `Sent`, `Delivered`, `Opened`, `Clicked`, `Unsubscribed`, `Bounced`, search, and rows or `No engagement yet`. A non-sent campaign must show `Campaign is not sent yet` and return to the list.
- **Retailer list.** Open `/account/seller/retailers`; require heading `Retailers`, `View Segments`, `Add Retailers`, and `Upload Retailers` or `View upload in progress`. Exercise `All`, `Uploaded`, `Invited`, `Signed Up`, `Sampled`, and `Ordered`, search `Search by name or email`, and applicable `Origin`, `Amount in Cart`, `State`, `Days since Invite`, `Days since Last Order`, and Sample Box filters. Wait for loading rows to resolve to rows or the invite-focused empty copy. On narrow view, use `More Actions`.
- **Retailer bulk actions.** Select only synthetic branch-local rows and require applicable `Create segment`, `Invite retailers`, and `Send reminders`. Open confirmation to verify counts, then cancel. Invites/reminders send customer-visible email and update lifecycle state; submit only with explicit authorization and verify both the refreshed row and queued/email outcome.
- **Retailer detail.** Open a selected `/account/seller/retailers/<id>` and require the visible retailer/prospect name plus lifecycle and `On Airgoods` or `Not On Airgoods` badges. For an on-Airgoods retailer, verify available `Amount spent`, `Orders`, `Avg. Order`, `In cart`, `Last order`, `Locations`, activity, products, orders, map/location, and `Notes`. For a prospect, verify prospect guidance plus state-appropriate `Edit`, `Verify email`, `Send Invite`, or `Send Reminder`. A missing fixture must show `Retailer not found.`. `j` and `k` navigate adjacent records only when focus is outside an input.
- **Segments.** Open `/account/seller/retailers/segments`; require `Retailer Segments`, `Suggest Segment`, `Create Segment`, `Create Campaign`, search, and rows or `Create a retailer segment to get started`. `Create Segment` opens `Create Segment from Filters`; verify `Segment Name`, `Description`, status/origin/state/cart/invite/last-order filters, `Calculating...`, and the live `retailers match` count. Zero matches must disable creation. On a detail route, require segment name, rows or the explicit no-retailers message, and `Create campaign`; non-default segments also expose `Delete`.
- **Notes.** Open `/account/seller/retailers/notes`; require `Notes`, `List`, `Grid`, `New Note`, `Search notes...`, and notes or `No notes found`. Open `New Note`; require `Customer`, `Title`, `Note`, `Cancel`, and `Add Note`, then cancel. On a retailer detail, the customer is preselected. Create/update/delete only a synthetic note, prove its resulting list/detail state, then delete or restore it.
- **Retailer upload.** Open `/account/seller/retailers/upload`; require `Retailer Upload`, the four steps `Upload File`, `Map Columns`, `Confirm Import`, `Import`, and `Select File`. Use a tiny synthetic CSV/TSV/XLSX with unique non-real addresses. Require file type/size errors when applicable; map required `Business Name` and `Email`, optionally `Contact Name`, inspect `Column Preview`, and require `Continue` disabled until required mappings exist. On confirmation, require `File Preview`, `Back`, and `Import`.
- **Upload status.** Submit only in isolated data with queue healthy. Require `Upload in progress`, a processed count, then `Upload completed!` with `Total processed`, `Uploaded`, `Duplicates`, and `Invalid`, or `Upload failed` with `Retry upload`. Prove added retailers through `View retailers` or a read-only database query. Delete synthetic imported rows and their synthetic segment afterward; do not expose the uploaded file or addresses in evidence.
- **Airgoods Direct.** Open `/account/seller/referrals`; require `Airgoods Direct`, `Send an Invite`, `Customize invite`, `CC me in the invite`, `Upload`, the saved `airgoods.com/u/<slug>` link, `Copy link for samples`, referral benefits, and `Your stats`. With a saved slug, require `View QR code` and the QR loading/success/error-and-retry state. Clipboard copy, QR open/copy/download, and opening the public Direct page are safe; sending an invite adds/updates a retailer and sends external email.
- **Direct slug and widget.** Opening slug edit must show `Changing your slug will make your previous direct link inactive`; cancel unless a restore is planned. At `/account/seller/referrals/widgets`, require `Embed an Airgoods Direct widget on your website`, `Step 1: Customize your widget`, size choices `Small`, `Regular`, `Large`, preview frame `Airgoods Direct Widget`, `Step 2: Copy the widget code`, `Send code to my developer`, and `Copy Widget Code`. Size is local preview state, but color changes autosave after a debounce and immediately affect installed widgets. Record and restore all four colors; developer email requires explicit authorization.
- **State and proof.** For every recipe capture loading plus populated, empty, validation/error, and success states that the selected fixture can safely reach. For writes, prove the result through a second user-facing read and a read-only database/API confirmation. For customer-visible discounts, Direct pages, or widgets, use a second buyer/public tab only with synthetic data. Record queue/email mode and distinguish API acceptance, queued work, provider acceptance, and recipient-visible delivery.

## Gotchas

- Core routes are not feature-flag gated, but navigation and optional recommendations vary by onboarding, placement availability, and seller demo flag. Direct navigation is valid proof only when the authenticated seller is authorized for the returned data.
- Admin impersonation is suitable for branch-local UI and reversible database writes. It is not proof for seller page/CTA/QR analytics, return-visit state, notification delivery, or any path that suppresses impersonated analytics. Use a real test seller session when analytics is the target.
- `/account/seller/retailers` keeps filter, search, page, page size, sort, and scroll state. Start from a recorded clean state or record the inherited state; `?source=invite-follow-up` applies a greater-than-21-days invite filter.
- Campaign composition autosaves as soon as valid draft state changes. Merely typing a campaign name can create a row and change the URL. Sent campaigns are not editable; deleting a campaign is irreversible.
- Campaign UI success proves persistence or enqueueing, not delivery. Campaign dispatch, recipient fan-out, unsubscribe/preferences checks, deliverability checks, and provider submission happen asynchronously. Local email-disabled mode cannot prove recipient-visible email.
- `TEST`, `ON_PAUSE`, and `CHURNED` suppliers can save drafts but cannot test-send, schedule, or send campaigns. Large sends over 1,000 recipients cannot use the Airgoods domain.
- Referral invites, reminders, test emails, live campaigns, scheduled campaigns, template requests, and widget-code emails contact people outside the browser. Use reserved test addresses and explicit authorization.
- Retailer upload stores the source file and creates queued records. Only one visible customer import should run at a time; an unhealthy queue leaves `Upload in progress` polling indefinitely. Never upload production contact lists or personal data.
- Discount creation/edit, tier saves, promo-event opt-in, CRM edits/deletes, segments, notes, slug changes, and widget colors are persistent. Discounts and widgets may be customer-visible immediately; always snapshot exact prior values and restore them.
- Sample Box discounts are generated by the program and are read-only here. Promo-event edit controls disappear once the event begins or the opt-in window closes.
- Changing the Direct slug invalidates old public and sample links. Widget embed code depends on the supplier UUID, while Direct links and QR codes depend on the saved slug; do not treat an unsaved slug draft as active.
- Widget preview content is inside an iframe and may not appear in the accessibility snapshot. Prove the outer controls, iframe title, network load, and screenshot without scripting inside the frame.
- CRM detail differs sharply between uploaded/invited prospects and on-Airgoods retailers. Do not report missing store-only stats, map, orders, or products as a defect on a prospect.
- Never capture real retailer emails, contact names, addresses, notes, campaign recipient lists, QR payloads, upload files, or message content in screenshots or video. Use synthetic fixtures or redact the evidence.
